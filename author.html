Meet the author / Conoce al autor

Luis M.
Minier.

Independent author · New York City

Luis M. Minier is a New York City–based independent author whose debut novel, Heralds of the Cemí, brings contemporary fantasy into conversation with Dominican diaspora life, Taíno ancestral memory, and the streets and families of the Bronx.

His storytelling explores identity, inherited history, community, and the importance of minds that experience the world differently. In Heralds of the Cemí, art and frequency become the language of a legacy that refuses to be forgotten.
